Words found

  • Tap two letters to swap them. Your turn ends when a row, a column, or a diagonal spells a new word.
  • Rows and diagonals read left to right, columns top to bottom.
  • Each new word scores 3 points. One swap can make several.
  • Each swap costs a point, but a turn never scores below zero.
  • The game ends when every word has been found.
